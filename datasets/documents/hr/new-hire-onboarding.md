# New Hire Onboarding Guide

Synthetic document for demonstration. Helio Systems, Inc. is a fictional company.

## Before day one

People Operations ships the laptop so it arrives at least two business days before the start date. IT creates the employee's identity account, and the hiring manager assigns an **onboarding buddy** from the same team.

## First week plan

**Day 1: Welcome and setup.** Attend the 09:30 welcome session, activate your identity account, enroll in multi-factor authentication (required before accessing any internal system, see SEC-001), and complete the HR portal profile and payroll forms.

**Day 2: Security and compliance.** Complete the mandatory security awareness training and the data-handling course. Both must be finished within the **first 5 business days**, or system access is suspended until they are done.

**Day 3: Team immersion.** Meet your buddy and teammates, read your team's charter and recent planning documents, and shadow at least one team ritual (stand-up, planning, or review).

**Day 4: Product deep dive.** Attend the product walkthrough and set up a personal sandbox workspace to explore the platform like a customer would.

**Day 5: Goals and first task.** Agree on a 30-60-90 day plan with your manager and pick up a small first task (a "starter ticket").

## 30-60-90 day plan

- **30 days:** understand the product, team processes, and key stakeholders. Ship the starter ticket.
- **60 days:** own a well-scoped piece of work end to end.
- **90 days:** contribute independently; complete the 90-day check-in with your manager and People Partner.

## Buddy program

Buddies meet new hires at least twice a week during the first month. The buddy is the first point of contact for "how do we do things here" questions; the manager remains responsible for goals and performance.

## Equipment and stipend

Claim the $500 home office stipend within your first 60 days through the expense tool. Receipts are required (see FIN-002).
