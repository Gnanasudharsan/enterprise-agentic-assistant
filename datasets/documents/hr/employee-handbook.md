# Employee Handbook

Synthetic document for demonstration. Helio Systems, Inc. is a fictional company.

## Working hours and core collaboration time

Helio is a remote-first company. Employees set their own schedules but must be available during **core collaboration hours, 10:00 to 14:00 in their team's primary time zone**, Monday to Friday. Customer-facing teams follow the on-call and shift rotations published by their managers.

## Paid time off (PTO)

Full-time employees receive **20 days of paid time off per calendar year**, accrued monthly (1.67 days per month). PTO is available from the first day of employment, but no more than 5 days may be taken during the first 90 days without manager approval.

- Up to **5 unused PTO days carry over** into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on March 31.
- PTO requests of 3 or more consecutive days must be submitted in the HR portal at least **two weeks in advance**.
- Unused PTO is not paid out on departure, except where local law requires it.

## Company holidays

Helio observes 11 company holidays per year, published each December for the following year. Employees working on a holiday because of an on-call rotation receive a replacement day off within 60 days.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees may take up to **10 days of paid sick leave per year**. A doctor's note is only required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days.

## Parental leave

All employees with at least 6 months of tenure are eligible for **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave**, which can be taken in up to two blocks within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Remote work and equipment

Employees may work from any country where Helio has a legal entity (United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Germany). Working from another country for more than **30 days in a calendar year** requires approval from People Operations, because of tax and employment-law implications.

New employees receive a company laptop and a one-time **home office stipend of $500**, claimed through the expense process (see FIN-002 Expense Policy).

## Code of conduct

Employees are expected to treat colleagues and customers with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are not tolerated. Concerns can be reported to a manager, to People Operations, or anonymously through the ethics hotline.
