# INC-204: Verification service timeouts delayed customer onboarding

Synthetic document for demonstration. Helio Systems, Inc. is a fictional company.

## Summary

Between **2026-09-08 and 2026-09-19**, a large share of identity verification (KYB) requests to our external verification provider, VeriTrust, timed out. Because verification became a blocking onboarding step on 2026-09-01 (CS-004 v2.0), affected customers could not get a workspace until verification succeeded on retry or was completed manually.

- **Severity:** SEV-2
- **Duration:** 12 days of degraded service
- **Customers affected:** 63 new customers had onboarding delayed by one or more business days
- **Support impact:** 118 support tickets tagged `kyb-verification`, compared with 9 in August

## Timeline

- **2026-09-01:** CS-004 v2.0 takes effect; verification becomes blocking.
- **2026-09-08:** Timeout rate on verification calls rises from under 1% to 22%.
- **2026-09-11:** Support escalates the growing ticket backlog to Platform Engineering (P2 under SUP-003).
- **2026-09-15:** Root cause identified.
- **2026-09-19:** Fix deployed; timeout rate returns below 1%.

## Root cause

The verification client was configured with a **5-second timeout and no retry with backoff**. After the September volume increase, VeriTrust applied per-tenant rate limiting, and queued requests regularly took 6 to 9 seconds. Our client gave up, marked the verification as failed, and required a manual restart by the onboarding specialist.

## Contributing factors

- The synchronous integration design chosen in ADR-012 meant every timeout blocked provisioning directly.
- There was no alert on verification timeout rate, so the issue was found through support ticket volume three days after it started.
- Onboarding specialists had no tooling to bulk-retry failed verifications.

## Remediation

1. Increased the timeout to 15 seconds and added exponential backoff with 3 retries (done).
2. Added an alert when the verification timeout rate exceeds 5% over 15 minutes (done).
3. Agreed a higher rate limit with VeriTrust (done).
4. Move verification to an **asynchronous queue** so provisioning can resume automatically when verification completes (planned for Q4 2026, tracked in ADR-012's follow-up).
