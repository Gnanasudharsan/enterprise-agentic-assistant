# Expense and Reimbursement Policy

Synthetic document for demonstration. Helio Systems, Inc. is a fictional company.

## General principles

Spend company money as if it were your own. Expenses must be business-related, reasonable, and submitted through the expense tool with an itemized receipt for any amount **over $25**.

## Submission deadlines

Submit expenses within **30 days** of the purchase. Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll run. Expenses submitted more than 90 days late are not reimbursed.

## Travel

- Business travel must be approved by your manager **before booking**.
- Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.
- Hotels: up to $250 per night in standard-cost cities and $350 per night in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich).

## Meals

The meal allowance while travelling is **$75 per day**. Team meals and customer entertainment require a cost-center approval from a director and must list attendees.

## Home office and equipment

New employees may claim a one-time **$500 home office stipend** within 60 days of their start date. After that, employees may claim up to $150 per year for ergonomic equipment.

## Software purchases

Do not buy software with personal or corporate cards. All software must go through the procurement and security review process (see SEC-001, section "Third-party software").

## Approval limits

| Amount | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to $1,000 | Manager |
| $1,000 to $10,000 | Director |
| Over $10,000 | VP and Finance |
